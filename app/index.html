How these deals were checked

NEW LOW STRONG PRICE ROUTINE SALE

NEW LOW — the lowest price the deal trackers have ever recorded for this product. STRONG PRICE — a deep cut, usually 40% or more, at or near the lowest tracked price. ROUTINE SALE — a genuine discount, but the same product is cheaper somewhere else, so it is not the deal it looks like. Each card says what the verdict is based on, and every card links straight to the product at its store.

Prices move all day during Black Friday. The sale price on the store page at checkout is the one that counts — if it has moved since this page was checked, the deal has moved too.
